NEW API ENDPOINTS
=====================================================================
GET  /api/realworld-data/weather
     Query: location=Colombo
     Returns: {source, location, temp, humidity, description, confidence}

GET  /api/realworld-data/price
     Query: symbol=BTC, asset_type=crypto|stock|auto
     Returns: {source, symbol, price_usd/price, change, confidence}

GET  /api/realworld-data/exchange-rate
     Query: from_currency=USD, to_currency=EUR
     Returns: {source, from, to, rate, confidence}

GET  /api/realworld-data/current-time
     Returns: {full, date, time, timezone, day, iso}

POST /api/verify-claim
     Body: {claim: string}
     Returns: {claim, verified, confidence, sources, reasoning}
